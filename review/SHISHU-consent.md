# Consent for a SHISHU call

\noindent\fcolorbox{JDNavy}{yellow!15}{\parbox{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}{\raggedright\small\textbf{Please read this first.} Lawyers will check the exact wording before the pilot, and before real users see it.}}

\vspace{0.7em}

This is the consent for one pre-consultation call. Please read it before you tick the box. The call will not start unless you agree.

## 1. What this call is

You are about to speak with SHISHU before you meet a doctor or a health worker. The call asks about your health and your child's health, so the person you meet can prepare. The call does not diagnose an illness, and it does not replace that visit.

## 2. What is recorded

SHISHU records your voice and the answers you give on this call.

## 3. Who can see it

Your voice and your answers are shared only with the doctor or health worker (ASHA/ANM) for this visit.

## 4. What SHISHU will not do

SHISHU will not sell this record. SHISHU will not pass it to anyone else to sell. SHISHU will not use your voice or your answers to build or train another product.

## 5. Your choice

You can refuse. If you do not agree, the call does not start, and you can still see the doctor or health worker.

You can stop the call at any time. Stopping the call does not take away that visit.

## Your agreement

By ticking the agreement box on the call screen, you say that you have read this page and you agree to this call on these terms.

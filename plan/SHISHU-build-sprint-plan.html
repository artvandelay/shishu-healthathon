Phase 1 · Week 1 · 5–11 Oct
Define the conversation spine
Bot behaviour spec
  • Spine: about 10 minutes of open conversation, then about 5 minutes of direct questions
  • Topics, follow-up triggers and red flags, mapped to our screening question bank
  • Escalation rules and what the bot must never say
  • Written twice: rules for engineers, a script for counsellors
Counselling session protocol
  • 2–3 counsellors, 10–20 sessions, all in Hindi
  • Pre-diagnosed mothers; disorder mix [TBC]
  • Same spine for everyone; each counsellor keeps their own style
  • Consent, recording, de-identification and transcript format
Joint, new: ground-truth study protocol — paired design, blinding, outcomes and consent, so approval clears before November.
Medical: curate the knowledge base (PID, rare-disease approach, counselling manuals); line up counsellors and mothers.
Tech: plan ingestion; shortlist Hindi speech tools; apply for WhatsApp Business and SIP; set up evaluation.

Phase 2 · Weeks 2–3 · 12–25 Oct
Record counselling sessions, build v0.1
Medical team
  • Run 10–20 Hindi sessions across 2–3 counsellors
  • Transcribe, with an English gloss
  • Annotate spine stage, follow-up moves and how key disclosures surfaced
  • Write role-play mother scenarios for week 4 [number TBC]
  • Draft the evaluation rubric
Tech team
  • Ingest the curated knowledge base
  • Implement the spine as prompt plus conversation-state logic
  • v0.1 web chat in Hindi, with full logging
  • Test Hindi speech-to-text and text-to-speech on recorded sessions
  • First draft of the PDF summary for the mother
Hand-off: first 3–5 transcripts reach tech by the end of week 2, not week 3, so style work can start early.

Phase 3 · Week 4 · 26 Oct – 1 Nov
Evaluate the prototype, fold in human style
Medical team
  • Run the role-play scenarios against v0.1
  • Score each on the rubric: safety, accuracy, warmth, natural Hindi, completeness of history
  • Log every unsafe or wrong answer as a must-fix
  • Agree which counselling moves the bot should copy
Tech team
  • Close must-fix issues first
  • Mine transcripts for phrasing, pacing and follow-up patterns
  • Add exemplars and style guidance; re-run the scenarios
  • Ship v0.2 on web for week-5 evaluation
Output: v0.2 that keeps the spec's spine but sounds like our counsellors, with a scored comparison against v0.1.

Ground-truth study
Measure the bot against a counsellor
Design
  • Each mother: bot session first, counsellor session back to back
  • A counsellor sits in on the bot session and can take over
  • [n] mothers across two one-week rounds
Rigour
  • The comparison counsellor did not sit in and does not see the bot's output
  • Randomise order in about half the pairs, if numbers allow, to control for rehearsal
  • Pilot-scale estimate, not a validation study
Outcomes
  • Red flags elicited
  • Completeness of history and pedigree
  • Agreement on the referral call
  • Where and why the bot missed or misread
At the finale: real agreement data between bot and counsellor, plus a list of failure modes to fix before V1.

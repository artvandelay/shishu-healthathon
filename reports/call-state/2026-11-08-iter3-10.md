## The mode

**A clinical fact logged on the next turn is refused when the child list was in the previous line.**

Call 178. She said `एक बच्चा है, दो साल का, और उसे झटके आते हैं।` No tool call on that turn. On `ठीक है।` the model sent only `topic.body_fits_milestones`, `child_1`. There was no child slot, so the gate skipped it and the batch raised `no applicable update`. The age was already said. The batch had forgotten the list.

A topic batch that does not include the child list opens the slots from the nearest earlier line that is about her child and states that many ages. A nearer line that states an age and is not about a child, such as a relative's age, does not open a slot.

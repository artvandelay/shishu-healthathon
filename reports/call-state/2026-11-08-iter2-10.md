## The mode

**A duration in months rewrites a year age.**

Call 161. She said `एक बच्चा है, दो साल का, और उसे झटके आते हैं।` No tool call on that turn. On `झटके छह महीने से आ रहे हैं।` the model sent the child with age `2`. The month-only rule saw the word महीने and stored 2 months. "छह महीने से" is how long the fits have lasted. It is not the child's age. The same rule also read that duration as an age of 0.5 years when it was looking for spoken ages.

A bare number becomes months only when that same number is a month age in the line. A phrase ending in से is a duration. It is not an age, and it does not retag one.

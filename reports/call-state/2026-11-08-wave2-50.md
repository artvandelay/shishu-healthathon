# Wave 2: 50 scenarios aimed at the gaps

Date: 2026-11-08
Calls: 95–144
Raw log: `reports/call-state/wave2-raw.jsonl`

Wave 1 left 17 calls structurally clean and never reached the control that the scenario was written for: which child, an unknown name, kinship versus a neighbor, a girl of 11, thalassemia after a living child, a stored miscarriage, operation reason, cord and BCG, growth, transfusion, fasting, NICU days, preterm and late, home birth, a correction after the bridge, "आधा साल", "पता नहीं" and "अगला सवाल" as the birth answer, emergency and caller-stop after a saved child, hearing normal versus not done, pregnancy conditions, cousins, an infant death phrased in plain words, and a boy and a girl. Wave 2 is those paths. It is not another 50 copies of one script.

## Result

35 of 50 failed a structural check. 15 did not. **No topic was wrongly `not_applicable`.** The calls 38–43 wipe still did not recur.

The same failure is still the majority. 30 calls saved no child list and stayed on `intro_children`. 16 of those also got `no applicable update` when the clinical topic arrived before any child record existed. Two more (calls 113 and 126) timed out in that same place; the child check is not applied on a timeout, so they are counted with the timeouts.
